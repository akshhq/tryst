About the Fest

A Legacy
Reborn

TRYST isn't just back - it's ascending. Hosted by Keshav Mahavidyalaya under the blazing banner of the University of Delhi, TRYST 2026 doesn't walk through the door - it kicks it wide open. This is the festival that doesn't ask for your attention, it commands it. From the first beat to the final bow, every moment is engineered to hit different louder, bolder, and more alive than anything you've witnessed before. With a diverse lineup spanning music, dance, art, theatre, and fashion, TRYST 2026 is a universe of its own built by students, powered by vision, and designed to leave you gawking.

With a diverse lineup spanning music, dance, art, fashion, and technology, TRYST continues to offer a space where ideas take shape and performances come to life. From competitive events to curated showcases, the festival reflects the energy and diversity of the student community.

TRYST steps into a world unlike any other Art Museum fused with Fantasy. The paintings breathe, sculptures move, and every exhibit pulses with an energy that defies reality as you know it. Ancient mythologies hang beside futuristic visions, surreal dreamscapes bleed into electrifying performances, and the familiar becomes fantastical at every turn. At TRYST 2026, every participant is both artist and audience you don't just walk through this gallery, you become part of it. The GALLERY is open. The art is alive. The realm is yours. And it's all waiting for you.

His vision to upgrade and expand opportunities reflects true dedication. We sincerely appreciate his efforts in making our campus a better place for everyone.

Why I Chose to Lead?

In My Own Words

कुछ दोस्त मिलते गए कारवां बनता गया !
हम साथ यूँ चलते रहे रास्ता बनता गया !!
I didn't choose to lead for the title, but to represent and be a voice for students. I've been on the other side too, just like everyone here. I've noticed the small things that matter, the ones we ignore, and what we wish would change—until it felt right to step up myself.

Message

A Note to Students

Time flies faster than we realize, so make sure you truly live and enjoy every moment. Before you know it, your final year will be here. Make the most of your college journey—these are the days you'll look back on. Have fun, be a little foolish, create lasting memories, but always stay grounded and respectful to those around you.

On behalf of the students, we extend our heartfelt gratitude for your dedicated journey as the Secretary of our college. What this fest means to me?

More Than an Event

This fest is more than just an event to me—it's something I've always wanted to build. I've watched it take shape through small ideas, long nights, and constant teamwork. Its real meaning lies in those moments of chaos, effort, and the quiet pride when everything finally comes together and the vision turns real.

Message

A Note to Students

To every student reading this, this fest is as much yours as it is ours. Some of your best memories will come from moments you didn't overthink. And if there's one thing I've learned as a student, it's that the college becomes special when you choose to be a part of it, so make it count.
